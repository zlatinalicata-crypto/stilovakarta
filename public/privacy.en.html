Why and how

The data is sent to the server only to calculate your result. We do not store it in a database and we do not build a profile of you. Your answers and result are kept in your browser’s storage on your device, so you do not have to start again.

The legal basis is the contract: without this data we cannot calculate the result.

The photo

The photo is processed only on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. Only the measured colour season is kept.
